Classifier-free guidance trains one network both with and without the condition \( c \). During training, \( c \) is replaced by a null token \( \varnothing \) at a small rate. At sampling time it sees the noisy input \( x_t \) twice, with \( c \) and without. The guidance scale \( w \) extrapolates away from the unconditional prediction:

\[ \tilde\epsilon = (1 + w)\,\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t, c) - w\,\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t, \varnothing) \] \[ \hat v = v_\theta(x_t, t, c) + w\,\big(v_\theta(x_t, t, c) - v_\theta(x_t, t, \varnothing)\big) \]

\( w = 0 \) is the plain conditional model, and the velocity form is the same rule. Two forward passes per step means guidance doubles the network calls.
