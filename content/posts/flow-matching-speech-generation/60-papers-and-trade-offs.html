Papers and Trade-offs at a Glance

A common shipped design is an autoregressive model that writes speech tokens and a flow matching or diffusion decoder that renders them for a vocoder. In Seed-TTS, deployed latency fell to 0.028 times the offline system's, with WER and similarity unchanged [15]. Flow matching became the common choice with Voicebox, and the table below groups the systems by family.

The table lines up the main speech generation systems by design choice and role, with rows grouped by family: diffusion, non-autoregressive flow matching, AR tokens plus a renderer, speech LLM decoders, and systems with no ODE at all.

Seed-TTS DiT [15] 2024 Non-autoregressive diffusion Vocoder latent Total duration supplied; alignment learned Non-streaming variant of Seed-TTS Simpler pipeline, but non-streaming
Voicebox [1] 2023 Non-autoregressive flow matching Mel, then HiFi-GAN Forced-aligned phones plus duration model Template for Audiobox and E2 TTS Depends on a phonemizer and forced aligner
Audiobox [22] 2023 Non-autoregressive flow matching Encodec latent Frame-aligned transcript plus flow duration model One model for speech, sound and music Broader coverage, but higher WER on LibriSpeech than Voicebox
Matcha-TTS [7] 2023 Non-autoregressive flow matching Mel, then HiFi-GAN MAS plus duration predictor Small single-speaker TTS Single speaker, deterministic durations
P-Flow [23] 2023 Non-autoregressive flow matching Mel, then HiFi-GAN MAS plus duration predictor; speech prompt in the text encoder Zero-shot TTS on small data Zero-shot duration prediction remains limited
VoiceFlow [24] 2023 Non-autoregressive rectified flow Mel, then HiFi-GAN Forced-alignment durations plus predictor Few-step TTS research Few-step quality needs an extra ReFlow training stage
E2 TTS [6] 2024 Non-autoregressive flow matching Mel, then BigVGAN-based vocoder Characters padded with filler tokens; alignment learned Reproduced and extended by F5-TTS Slow convergence; fragile alignment in reproductions
F5-TTS [3] 2024 Non-autoregressive flow matching Mel, then Vocos Filler-padded characters refined by ConvNeXt V2; length from a text ratio Public-data baseline in CosyVoice 2 comparisons Long mel sequences; no fine-grained emotion control
Seed-TTS [15] 2024 AR tokens, then diffusion Speech tokens, then vocoder latent Implicit in the AR language model Deployed as a streaming system Two-stage pipeline; its own DiT variant scores better on English
CosyVoice [25] 2024 AR tokens, then flow matching Semantic tokens, then mel Implicit in the AR LLM Template for the GLM-4-Voice decoder Token sampling variance between runs
CosyVoice 2 [13] 2024 AR tokens, then chunk-aware causal flow matching 25 Hz tokens, then mel Implicit in the AR LM Streaming TTS; tokenizer reused by Step-Audio 2 Streaming loses some accuracy on hard sentences
CosyVoice 3 [26] 2025 AR tokens, then flow-matching DiT 25 Hz tokens, then mel Implicit in the AR LM In-the-wild multilingual TTS; reward applied to the token stage Reward lowers error rates while similarity slips
GLM-4-Voice [27] 2024 LLM tokens, then flow matching 12.5 Hz tokens, then mel and HiFi-GAN Implicit in the LLM End-to-end spoken chatbot Decoder adapted for streaming inference
Step-Audio [28] 2025 LLM tokens, then flow matching Dual-codebook tokens, then mel and vocoder Implicit in the LLM Speech interaction model Adds a 3B language model inside the speech decoder
Step-Audio 2 [29] 2025 LLM tokens, then flow matching CosyVoice 2 tokens, then mel and HiFi-GAN Implicit in the LLM Speech interaction model Extra CNN encoder layer after each self-attention module
Kimi-Audio [30] 2025 LLM tokens, then flow matching 12.5 Hz tokens, then 50 Hz mel and BigVGAN Implicit in the LLM Speech output of an audio LLM Needs look-ahead to avoid chunk-boundary artefacts
Qwen2.5-Omni [31] 2025 LLM tokens, then flow-matching DiT Codec tokens, then mel and modified BigVGAN Implicit in the LLM Speech output of an omni-modal LLM Block attention trades look-ahead against latency
MaskGCT [32] 2024 Masked token prediction, no ODE Semantic tokens, then codec tokens None; total length predicted or given Baseline in F5-TTS and CosyVoice 2 comparisons Predicted length costs accuracy; many sequential passes
Moshi [33] 2024 Codec tokens, no flow decoder Mimi codec tokens Implicit in the model Real-time spoken dialogue Streams by design, but quality is bounded by the codec
Qwen3-Omni [34] 2025 LLM tokens, then causal ConvNet, no flow decoder Multi-codebook codec frames Implicit in the LLM Speech output of an omni-modal LLM Dropped block-wise diffusion to cut first-packet latency

Five axes separate these systems, and the "what breaks" column is my read of the papers. Grad-TTS shows gains flattening after about 10 steps, and CFG doubles the forward passes behind every step.
