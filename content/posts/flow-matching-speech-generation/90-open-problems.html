Open Problems

  • Rewards for the flow stage: A deterministic ODE sampler has no likelihood for a policy gradient, so current practice rewards the token model or duration predictor instead. One route turns the ODE into an SDE, which also changes the sampler.
  • The few-step floor: Schedules are usable to about 7 steps and break below about 6. Students go further but lose diversity and can learn to game the evaluator.
  • Evaluation that saturates: Some top systems already beat ground-truth recordings on WER, and predicted MOS stops agreeing with listeners once both clips are clean. Neither WER nor SIM measures diversity.
