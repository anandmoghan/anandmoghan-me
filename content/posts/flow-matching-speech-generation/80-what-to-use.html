Techniques Simplified: What to Use?

Start from the constraint you face first; each row gives a common choice and what it costs.

Situation What to use What it costs
Zero-shot TTS from scratch Non-autoregressive flow on mel, masked infilling, padded characters, total length only Slow convergence, fragile alignment, a separate vocoder
Decoder under a speech LLM AR semantic tokens, then a flow decoder to mel, a common choice among shipped systems A second model to train and serve; token sampling variance
Streaming under a latency budget One decoder trained under chunk-aware causal masks, with a few tokens of look-ahead Seams at chunk edges; every look-ahead frame paid in first-packet time
Cutting serving cost A warped or pruned schedule with Euler, which holds quality down to about 7 steps; then a distilled student with guidance folded in Lost diversity, metrics that flatter the student, and the vocoder, often the next bottleneck
Hardening quality Fixed evaluators, rewards on the token model or duration predictor, human preferences for prosody Reward hacking, slipping speaker similarity, human labelling
