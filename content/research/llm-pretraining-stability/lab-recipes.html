Lab recipes for pretraining stability

Research note, 2024 to 2026 open technical reports. Source for the LLM pretraining stability post.

How to read this note

confirmed means I read the statement in the primary source (report or first-party page). reported means it came from a search summary or a secondary page and was not checked against the primary text. inference is my own reading across sources.

Long reports were fetched through a summarising tool, and for most of them only the first part of the text was read. A missing statement below therefore means "not seen", not "not in the report".

Per-report notes

OLMo 2 (2501.00656)

  • Two problems are named: sudden spikes in loss and (more often) gradient norm, and slow growth of gradient-norm magnitude over the run. Larger models spiked more, and big gradient-norm spikes often preceded loss spikes. confirmed [OLMo 2 Furious, 2501.00656, Section 3]
  • Spike score is the share of values at least 7 standard deviations from a rolling average of the last 1,000 values. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.2]
  • Data: documents with a sequence of 32 or more repeated n-grams (1 to 13 tokens) are removed, and such sequences are also masked in the loss. This mitigated gradient spikes "though not complete elimination" and did not affect the slow gradient-norm growth. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.1]
  • Init: every parameter from N(0, 0.02) replaced the scaled init of OLMo-0424. Gradient-norm spike score fell from 0.40 to 0.03 in the ablation, at the cost of slightly slower convergence. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.2]
  • Reordered norm and QK-norm help only together: "In isolation, neither of these changes yield good results". Together the gradient spike score went from 0.108 to 0.069. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.3.2]
  • Z-loss: a penalty on the softmax normaliser log Z squared, to stop final-softmax activations growing. The text says weight 1e-4 while Table 1 lists 1e-5 (see open questions). confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.3.3]
  • Z-loss implementation detail: two implementations agreed in the forward pass but differed in the backward pass, and the team retrained from the divergence point. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.3.3]
  • AdamW epsilon lowered from 1e-5 to 1e-8; the gradient norm "settles much more quickly and remains permanently lower". confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.4.1]
  • No weight decay on embeddings: decay shrinks embeddings and can cause large early-layer gradients. Spike score 0.16 with decay, 0.092 without. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 3.4.2]
  • A peak LR of 30e-4 showed instability already during warmup, with spikes that did not fully recover, so it was abandoned. confirmed [2501.00656, Section 4.1]

Olmo 3 (2512.13961)

  • Modelling and training "largely follows that of OLMo 2"; the new architectural item is sliding-window attention in three of every four layers, last layer always full. confirmed [Olmo 3, 2512.13961, Sections 3 and 3.2]
  • No loss-spike or QK-norm discussion appeared in the first 100k characters read; the appendix tables were not read. confirmed (about what I read, not about the whole paper)

Llama 3 (2407.21783)

  • "We found this training recipe to be very stable: we observed few loss spikes" and no interventions for divergence were needed. confirmed [The Llama 3 Herd of Models, 2407.21783, Section 3.4.1]
  • Recipe: peak LR 8e-5 for 405B (3e-4 for 8B, 1.5e-4 for 70B), 8,000-step linear warmup, cosine decay to 8e-7 over 1.2M steps. confirmed [2407.21783, Sections 3.2 and 3.4.1]
  • Batch schedule: 4M tokens (4,096 sequence length), doubled to 8M after 252M tokens, then to 16M after 2.87T tokens, "to improve training stability" early and efficiency later. confirmed [2407.21783, Section 3.4.1]
  • FP32 gradient accumulation and FP32 reduce-scatter were used after fixing "several numerical issues". confirmed [2407.21783, Section 3]
  • Reliability is the larger problem: 466 interruptions in a 54-day window (419 unexpected), about 78% attributed to hardware, 6 silent data corruption events. Effective training time stayed above 90%. confirmed [2407.21783, Section 3.3.4]
  • Mid-training mix changes included downsampling data later found to be lower quality. confirmed [2407.21783, Section 3.4]

DeepSeek-V3 (2412.19437)

  • "We did not experience any irrecoverable loss spikes or perform any rollbacks" over the full run. confirmed [DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report, 2412.19437, abstract and introduction]
  • Auxiliary-loss-free balancing: a per-expert bias adjusted from batch-level load, speed 0.001 for the first 14.3T tokens then 0; plus a tiny sequence-wise balance loss (alpha 0.0001). confirmed [2412.19437, Sections 2.1.2 and 4.2]
  • Each token is routed to at most 4 nodes. confirmed [2412.19437, Section 4.2]
  • FP8 training: relative loss error stays below 0.25%; outlier-prone components keep higher precision. Master weights and gradients stay FP32; AdamW moments are BF16. confirmed [2412.19437, Sections 3.3 to 3.3.3]
  • AdamW with betas (0.9, 0.95), weight decay 0.1, gradient clip 1.0. confirmed [2412.19437, Section 4.2]
  • The report does not attribute the stability to any single item. inference

Kimi K2 (2507.20534)

  • Attention-logit explosion is the named instability, more frequent with Muon than AdamW in their experiments. A 9B-active, 53B-total run with vanilla Muon saw max logits "quickly exceed a magnitude of 1000". confirmed [Kimi K2: Open Agentic Intelligence, 2507.20534]
  • Soft-capping is called insufficient (the dot product can grow before the cap applies), and QK-norm is incompatible with MLA because keys are not materialised at inference. confirmed [2507.20534]
  • QK-Clip rescales query and key projection weights after the update when a head's max logit exceeds tau; it does not change the current step's forward or backward pass, and is applied per head. K2 used tau 100; logits decayed to a stable range after about 30% of training. confirmed [2507.20534]
  • Claim: 15.5T tokens "without a single loss spike". The figure omits the very beginning of training "for clarity". confirmed [2507.20534, abstract and Figure 3 caption]
  • Recipe: LR 2e-4 constant for 10T tokens, cosine to 2e-5 over 5.5T, batch 67M tokens, weight decay 0.1. confirmed [2507.20534]

Qwen3 (2505.09388)

  • QK-norm added "to ensure stable training"; QKV-bias from Qwen2 removed. confirmed [Qwen3 Technical Report, 2505.09388]
  • MoE variants: 128 experts, 8 active, no shared experts, global-batch load-balancing loss. confirmed [2505.09388]
  • No loss-spike discussion seen in the part read. confirmed

Gemma 2 and Gemma 3 (2408.00118, 2503.19786)

  • Gemma 2: tanh soft-capping at 50.0 for attention logits and 30.0 for final logits; RMSNorm on inputs and outputs of each sub-layer "to stabilize training". confirmed [Gemma 2, 2408.00118]
  • Gemma 3: "we replace the soft-capping of Gemma 2 with QK-norm", citing Wortsman et al. on small-scale proxies for instability. Neither report includes a stability ablation. confirmed [Gemma 3 Technical Report, 2503.19786]
  • Gemma 2 to Gemma 3 is a lab-level swap of soft-capping for QK-norm, which fits the field converging on QK-norm. inference

MiniMax-01 and MiniMax-M1 (2501.08313, 2506.13585)

  • MiniMax-01 uses DeepNorm scaling (alpha (2N)^0.25, beta (8N)^-0.25) and Xavier init, noting "PostNorm can be prone to vanishing and exploding gradients"; AdamW betas (0.9, 0.95), weight decay 0.1. confirmed [MiniMax-01, 2501.08313, Sections 2.3 and 4.2]
  • MiniMax-M1 is an RL-stage story: the LM head was raised to FP32 after high-magnitude activations caused a training/inference probability mismatch (correlation from about 0.9x to 0.99x). confirmed [MiniMax-M1, 2506.13585, Section 3.2]
  • Same report: gradients span 1e-18 to 1e-5, so the default AdamW (0.9, 0.999, eps 1e-8) caused non-convergence; they set betas (0.9, 0.95) and eps 1e-15. confirmed [2506.13585, Section 3.2]
  • Long-context extension: overly aggressive length increases "can lead to a sudden gradient explosion". confirmed [2506.13585, Section 2.1]

Marin 8B and 32B (first-party retrospectives)

  • 8B: "fairly stable with very few spikes"; peak LR 1e-3 chosen because 2e-3 (suggested by the DCLM paper) was unstable. confirmed [Marin 8B Retrospective, marin.readthedocs.io/en/latest/reports/marin-8b-retro/]
  • 8B skipped z-loss at first; during deep cooldown the loss crept up and tracking norms showed "the lm_head was exploding". Resetting optimizer state and removing weight decay did not fix it; adding z-loss 1e-4 did. confirmed [Marin 8B Retrospective]
  • 32B: "many more loss spikes than we had seen at 8b (and in 70b trials)". Tried max grad norm 0.2, update-norm clipping, skip-bad-steps (sigma 2.0, window 128), optimizer-state rebuild and Muon. "Gradient clipping and step-skipping heuristics softened the spikes but never removed them." confirmed [Marin 32B Retrospective, marin.readthedocs.io/en/latest/reports/marin-32b-retro/]
  • Fix: warm-start from the step-80k Llama weights into the Qwen3 32B architecture (adds QK-norm) with a 1,000-step re-warmup. One-time loss penalty, recovery in about 10B tokens, "the loss spikes disappeared entirely"; "We should have tested QK-Norm earlier." confirmed [Marin 32B Retrospective]
  • Data side: near step 190k the training loss phase-shifted while validation stayed flat; the team attributes it to correlated batches from the affine shuffle and fixed it with a Feistel shuffle plus cleaner math data. confirmed [Marin 32B Retrospective]

GLM-4.5 (2508.06471)

  • Muon for all parameters except embeddings, biases and RMSNorm weights (5 Newton-Schulz steps, momentum 0.95, update RMS scaled to 0.2). confirmed [GLM-4.5, 2508.06471, Section 2]
  • QK-norm "to stabilize the range of attention logits" (on in GLM-4.5, off in Air); loss-free balance routing with bias update 0.001 for 15T tokens then 0. confirmed [2508.06471, Section 2]

Ling family (2503.05139, 2510.22115) and LongCat-Flash (2509.01322)

  • Ling (300B MoE): narrow spikes last a few steps and matter little; wide spikes persist and can push benchmarks toward chance. Causes are "specific interactions between the data and optimizer states". Mitigation: skip the update, re-inject the data later, and cut the LR if it persists. confirmed [Every FLOP Counts, 2503.05139, Section 3.4.4]
  • Same report: router z-loss coefficient 1e-4, balance loss 0.015, stochastic routing warmup against early expert collapse, and NormHead because "the output norm of the LM-Head often becomes unstable, particularly during loss spikes". confirmed [2503.05139, Sections 3.2 and 3.4]
  • Ling 2.0: first layers dense "for stability", QK-norm that "significantly improve[s] stability under low-precision training", init std 0.006, clip 1.0, FP8 gap at most 0.25% after 900B tokens, top-32 checkpoint merging (WSM). confirmed [Ling 2.0, 2510.22115]
  • LongCat-Flash: "massive activations correlate with severe loss spikes", hence a hidden z-loss on large-magnitude activations. Also width-scaled hyperparameter transfer, a monitored router gradient ratio, model-growth init from a 14-layer model, deterministic kernels and silent-data-corruption checks; "no irrecoverable loss spikes". confirmed [LongCat-Flash Technical Report, 2509.01322]

Nemotron-H, SmolLM3, Apertus, OLMoE, Hunyuan-Large

  • Nemotron-H: FP8 loss within 0.1% of BF16; first and last 4 GEMMs left in BF16 "for training stability and convergence"; FP8 results at small scale did not carry to larger models. confirmed [Nemotron-H, 2504.03624]
  • SmolLM3: removes embedding weight decay "following OLMo 2", intra-document masking for "faster and more stable long context training", clip 1, WSD; no spike story. confirmed [SmolLM3 blog, huggingface.co/blog/smollm3]
  • Apertus: QK-norm "preventing excessively large attention logits", pre-norm RMSNorm, WSD that avoids re-warming, fixed beginning-of-document token with document masking said to improve stability. confirmed [Apertus, 2509.14233]
  • OLMoE ablations: router z-loss (weight 0.001) gives fewer spikes and lower loss at about 2% throughput cost; QK-norm prevents grad-norm spikes at nearly 10% throughput cost; non-parametric layer norm causes many gradient spikes; normal init diverged near 450B tokens while truncated normal was stable. confirmed [OLMoE, 2409.02060, Sections 4.1 and 4.2]
  • Hunyuan-Large: recycle routing for tokens sent to overloaded experts because token dropping "may negatively impact training stability"; expert LR scaled by about 0.31. confirmed [Hunyuan-Large, 2411.02265]

Data-side causes and fixes

  • Repeated or degenerate sequences: filter documents with 32 or more repeated n-grams and mask them in the loss. Partial fix only. confirmed [OLMo 2, 2501.00656]
  • Spikes are not purely "bad data": PaLM replayed the batches around spikes from an earlier checkpoint and saw no spike, so it concluded in favour of a data batch times parameter state interaction. confirmed [PaLM, 2204.02311, Section 5.1]
  • PaLM's practice: restart about 100 steps before the spike and skip roughly 200 to 500 batches. confirmed [2204.02311]
  • Ling's version: skip the update and re-inject the data later rather than discard it. confirmed [2503.05139]
  • Data ordering: Marin's loss phase shift traced to a weak shuffle ("Shuffle quality matters even given perfect per-domain shuffling"). confirmed [Marin 32B Retrospective]
  • Mix changes mid-run: Llama 3 downsampled lower-quality subsets; Marin changed mixtures and rewarmed with no spike. confirmed [2407.21783; Marin 8B Retrospective]
  • Cross-document attention: Llama 3, SmolLM3 and Apertus mask across document boundaries; SmolLM3 and Apertus tie it to stability, Llama 3's link is to long-context training. reported (Llama 3 statement not re-checked in the text)

Defaults versus lab-specific

Near-universal defaults in 2025 reports

  • Pre-norm RMSNorm blocks, with a second post-sublayer norm in Gemma and OLMo 2 (OLMo 2 moves the norm after the sublayer). inference
  • QK-norm: OLMo 2, OLMoE, Gemma 3, Qwen3, GLM-4.5, Ling 2.0, Apertus, Marin 32B (after spikes). Exceptions in the other direction: Llama 3 had no QK-norm and was stable; Kimi K2 cannot use it with MLA and built QK-Clip instead. inference
  • AdamW with beta2 0.95 (DeepSeek-V3, MiniMax, GLM-family Muon aside), weight decay 0.1, gradient clip 1.0. inference
  • Small init (std 0.02 or smaller; Ling 0.006) instead of scaled init. inference
  • High-precision islands in low-precision training: FP32 accumulation, FP32 master weights and gradients, BF16 first and last GEMMs, FP32 LM head. inference
  • Monitoring max attention logits and gradient norm as spike precursors is implicit in OLMo 2, Kimi K2 and LongCat-Flash. inference

Lab-specific or still contested

  • Z-loss: OLMo 2, OLMoE, Marin (later), Ling router z-loss; not named by Llama 3, DeepSeek-V3, Qwen3 or Gemma 3 in what I read. inference
  • Embedding weight decay removal: OLMo 2 and SmolLM3 only. inference
  • Logit soft-capping: Gemma 2 only, dropped in Gemma 3. inference
  • QK-Clip and MuonClip: Kimi K2 (the Muon-specific problem); GLM-4.5 uses Muon with QK-norm instead. inference
  • Hidden z-loss, model-growth init, deterministic kernels and SDC checks: LongCat-Flash. confirmed [2509.01322]
  • Aux-loss-free balancing is DeepSeek-led; Qwen3 chose a global-batch balance loss; Ling uses balance plus router z-loss. confirmed [2412.19437; 2505.09388; 2503.05139]
  • Spike handling at run time (skip, rewind, re-inject, lower LR) is practised but rarely documented in recent reports; PaLM and Ling are the clearest first-party accounts. inference

Open questions and conflicting sources

  • OLMo 2 z-loss weight: Section 3.3.3 says 1e-4 while Table 1 lists 1e-5. Do not quote a number without checking the PDF. confirmed [2501.00656]
  • Z-loss evidence is mixed: Marin's 1.4B test found no discernible effect on loss or evals and showed it raises the lm_head norm, while Marin 8B found it fixed cooldown lm_head blow-up and OLMoE found fewer spikes. reported (the 1.4B result came via a search summary of a W&B report)
  • Marin 32B retrospective phase table is internally inconsistent (phase boundaries at 80,000 vs the 70k to 80k instability window). Whether QK-norm alone, or the architecture switch plus re-warmup, removed the spikes was not isolated, since the earlier fixes ran on the same weights. confirmed [Marin 32B Retrospective]
  • Kimi K2's "zero spikes" figure omits the start of training, and ablations showing QK-Clip does not hurt quality sit in an appendix I could not see. confirmed [2507.20534]
  • Llama 3 reports few spikes with no QK-norm, small peak LR and a batch ramp, and DeepSeek-V3 gives no attribution, so the evidence that any single technique "lets labs go bigger" is mostly ablation at small scale plus anecdote. inference
  • Not verified: DeepSeek-V3.x, Qwen3-Next, MiniMax-M2, Step-3, Nemotron 4 340B, and the tail of the OLMo 3, Apertus (Section 2.6 retrospective) and Ling 2.0 reports. The search did not return primary texts for the first four. confirmed
  • Secondary summaries claimed Ling-1T was FP8 with a gap under 0.25%; the Ling 2.0 primary text confirms 0.25% after 900B tokens, so quote the primary wording. confirmed [2510.22115]
  • Gemma 3 report cites Wortsman et al. (2023) for QK-norm but states no motivation beyond replacing soft-capping, so "Gemma moved for stability" is my reading. inference
