  • Title: Flow Matching for Speech Generation
  • Excerpt: From diffusion to flow matching: how modern TTS models are trained, how they sample fast enough to stream, and where they still break.
  • Tags: machine-learning, deep-learning, speech (new tag to add to DEFAULT_TAGS).
  • published: false

Angle: flow matching has become the default acoustic generator in modern TTS and speech LLM decoders. The reader is an ML engineer who knows transformers but has not shipped a diffusion model. Voice and depth match the RL in LLMs and attention posts: practitioner tone, equations with plain-language interpretation, tables for comparisons, one real trade-off or failure mode per section.
