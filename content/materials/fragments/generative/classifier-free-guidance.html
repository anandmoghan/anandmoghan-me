Classifier-free guidance (CFG)

CFG pushes the output toward the condition \( c \) by extrapolating away from an unconditional prediction \( \varnothing \). The guidance scale \( w \) sets the strength.

\[ \hat v = v_\theta(x,t,c) + w\,\big(v_\theta(x,t,c) - v_\theta(x,t,\varnothing)\big) \]

The cost is two forward passes per sampling step, so CFG doubles inference compute. The same form applies to a diffusion network by swapping \( v_\theta \) for \( \epsilon_\theta \).

Classifier-free guidance was introduced for diffusion models (arXiv 2207.12598); the form above applies it to a velocity network.
