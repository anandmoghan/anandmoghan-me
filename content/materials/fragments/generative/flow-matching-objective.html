Conditional flow matching objective

Flow matching regresses a network onto a velocity field. With a linear (optimal-transport) path, \( x_0 \) is noise and \( x_1 \) is data.

\[ x_t = (1-t)\,x_0 + t\,x_1, \qquad \mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}\,\big\|v_\theta(x_t, t) - (x_1 - x_0)\big\|^2 \]

The target velocity \( x_1 - x_0 \) is constant along the path, so training is plain regression. There is no noise schedule and no SNR weighting.

Sampling integrates the learned ODE from noise to data:

\[ \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = v_\theta(x, t) \]

The linear path and velocity regression follow Flow Matching with optimal-transport paths (arXiv 2210.02747).
