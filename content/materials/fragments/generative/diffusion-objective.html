Diffusion objective (DDPM)

The forward process mixes clean data \( x_0 \) with Gaussian noise \( \epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \). The network \( \epsilon_\theta \) learns to predict that noise from the noisy input and the timestep \( t \).

\[ x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon, \qquad \mathcal{L} = \mathbb{E}\,\big\|\epsilon - \epsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\big\|^2 \]

Here \( \bar\alpha_t \) is the cumulative noise schedule, so it fixes how much signal survives at step \( t \). Sampling runs the learned denoiser backwards from pure noise.

This is the noise-prediction objective of the DDPM paper (arXiv 2006.11239).
