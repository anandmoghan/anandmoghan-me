Six sentences to remember

Inference My synthesis of the table, not a sourced claim.

  1. Several systems here run audio at about 12.5 Hz (Moshi, GLM-4-Voice, Kimi-Audio, Step-Audio 2, Qwen3-Omni).
  2. Output is often an LM for content plus a flow or ConvNet renderer.
  3. Text is the scaffold and the supervision.
  4. Duplex looks limited by data and evaluation more than by architecture.
  5. Latency comes from token rate, NFE and chunking.
  6. In 2026 avatar work, joint audio-video teachers (Ovi, LTX-2) are distilled into streaming students, and the open gap is avatars that listen.
