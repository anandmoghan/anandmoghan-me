Full-duplex models compared

A full-duplex model listens while it speaks. Backchannels, overlaps and barge-in then come from the model's own decisions, not from a VAD timer. The sources group the designs by where the "speak or listen" decision lives.

Survey levels L0 to L3

A 2026 survey (arXiv 2606.19453) defines four levels. L0 is an external scheduler around a half-duplex model. L1 is a control head reading the LLM's hidden states. L2 encodes the duties in the token stream itself. L3 is representation-level and not realised yet. Its line to remember: "architecture sets capacity, training realizes it".

Inference My mapping: control-head models are L1; dual-stream, flattened, chunked, action-channel and special-token models are L2.

Control head Freeze-Omni (arXiv 2411.00774) Frozen LLM; chunk-wise state prediction from the LLM's last layer: keep receiving speech, or the chunk ends the user's speech (with or without interrupting) 745 ms total latency in the authors' latency breakdown

Latency is not comparable across this table

Moshi's 160 ms is theoretical; MinMo gives theory and practice; Freeze-Omni averages total latency; PersonaPlex and DuplexSLA report turn-taking delays on their own tests. Hardware and definitions differ. Quote a number with its definition, never as a ranking.

Reported Most figures are authors' own. On DuplexSLA-Bench (self-reported, arXiv 2605.20755), backchannel accuracy is 0.33% for gpt-realtime-1.5 (semantic-vad-high), 40.00% for gemini-3.1-flash-live and 98.33% for DuplexSLA. Instruct-FD (arXiv 2607.20460) finds the best of six full-duplex systems follows turn-taking instructions 64.4% of the time.
