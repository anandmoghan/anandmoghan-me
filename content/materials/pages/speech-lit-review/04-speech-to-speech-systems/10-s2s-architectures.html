S2S architectures with the LLM as a downstream component

Six families, ordered by how tightly speech is fused into the LLM, plus a seventh that pairs two models. The first three are the most conservative designs; the next three fuse speech into the LLM most tightly. The tandem family sits outside that ordering: a small real-time model keeps the floor while a larger model supplies knowledge.

The seven families

Family Models How text and speech are produced together
Cascaded ASR, LLM, TTS Sequential; latencies add (Moshi, arXiv 2410.00037, cites a latency of several seconds)
Half-cascade LLaMA-Omni 2 (arXiv 2505.02625), Freeze-Omni (arXiv 2411.00774) Speech encoder into text LLM; a streaming speech decoder consumes LLM text or hidden states
Thinker-Talker Qwen3-Omni (arXiv 2509.17765), Qwen3.5-Omni (arXiv 2604.15804) Thinker produces text; Talker conditions on it and emits speech codes
Interleaved GLM-4-Voice (arXiv 2412.02612), Step-Audio 2 (arXiv 2507.16632), VITA-Audio (arXiv 2505.03739) One sequence alternates text and audio tokens
Parallel streams Moshi (arXiv 2410.00037), Kimi-Audio (arXiv 2504.18425) Moshi: text tokens run time-aligned with audio tokens (Inner Monologue). Kimi-Audio: shared transformer layers, then parallel text and audio heads
Codec-free SALMONN-omni (arXiv 2411.18138) Embeddings instead of codec tokens; a "thinking" mechanism facilitates asynchronous text and speech generation
Tandem KAME (arXiv 2510.02327), MoshiRAG (arXiv 2604.12928), DuplexOmni (arXiv 2606.09186) A compact real-time speech model answers immediately. A second, larger module (back-end LLM, retriever or pluggable thinking layer) works asynchronously and feeds text or results back into the speech model's output

Key facts per model

  • LLaMA-Omni 2: 0.5B to 14B on Qwen2.5, Whisper-large-v3 encoder, a streaming speech decoder inspired by CosyVoice 2, trained on 200K multi-turn dialogues; the authors report beating GLM-4-Voice (arXiv 2505.02625).
  • Freeze-Omni: frozen LLM, so the authors report speech-mode intelligence at the same level as the text mode of the backbone, plus chunk-wise state prediction for duplex dialogue (arXiv 2411.00774).
  • Qwen3-Omni: text in 119 languages, speech understanding in 19, speech generation in 10 (arXiv 2509.17765).
  • Qwen3.5-Omni (Apr 2026): hybrid-attention MoE Thinker and Talker, 256k context, and ARIA, which dynamically aligns text and speech units to stabilise streaming speech and prosody (arXiv 2604.15804). The authors claim SOTA on 215 audio and audio-visual subtasks.
  • Kimi-Audio: continuous audio features in, discrete tokens out, more than 13M h of audio pretraining (arXiv 2504.18425).
  • MiniCPM-o 2.6: 8B, built from SigLip-400M, Whisper-medium-300M, ChatTTS-200M and Qwen2.5-7B, with a time-division multiplexing mechanism for streaming (OpenBMB model card).
  • Commercial: OpenAI's Realtime API model "works directly with audio" (OpenAI docs). Google's Live API offers native audio output models (Gemini API docs).
  • KAME: the S2S transformer processes user speech for immediate responsiveness while the query is relayed to a back-end LLM; the LLM's text response is injected in real time to guide speech generation. On a speech-synthesized variant of MT-Bench the authors report correctness approaching a cascade, at latency on par with the S2S baseline (arXiv 2510.02327).
  • MoshiRAG: a compact full-duplex model identifies knowledge-demanding queries and grounds the answer in retrieved information. Retrieval finishes inside the natural gap between the start of a response and the delivery of its core information. The authors report factuality comparable to the best publicly released non-duplex speech LMs, and retrieval methods can be swapped without retraining (arXiv 2604.12928).
  • DuplexOmni: an interaction layer handles streaming audio and video and speaks in real time; a pluggable thinking layer provides reasoning and tool use; the two run asynchronously in parallel. A Writer-Director pipeline builds its continuous-interaction training data (arXiv 2606.09186).
  • Mind-Paced Speaking: a "Formulation Brain" reasons and paces a separate "Articulation Brain" that speaks. Under a zero-latency configuration the authors report 92.8% on Spoken-MQA and 82.5 on URO-Bench, and say the method underlies their released Step-Audio R1.1 (arXiv 2510.09592).
  • Stream RAG: tool queries are predicted while the user is still speaking. On AudioCRAG the authors report QA accuracy rising from 11.1% to 34.2% absolute and tool-use latency down 20% (arXiv 2510.02044).
  • Gemini 3.8 Live (first-party model page, updated 2026-09-15): asynchronous function calling (NON_BLOCKING) is the default, scheduling modes are SILENT, WHEN_IDLE and INTERRUPTED, reasoning is interleaved, proactive audio is permanently on, and the input limit is 131,072 tokens (model page).
  • gpt-realtime-2 (first-party model page): configurable reasoning effort, where higher effort "can increase latency and output token usage", a 128,000-token context window, 32,000 max output tokens and function calling (model page).

The intelligence gap

Speaking costs reasoning quality. Causes reported: catastrophic forgetting and modality inequivalence (arXiv 2509.14930), and a bottleneck in condensing redundant speech into stable late-layer decisions (arXiv 2603.01502). Mitigations: a frozen LLM (arXiv 2411.00774), cross-modal distillation from a text teacher (arXiv 2509.14930), layer-wise hidden-state alignment with RL (arXiv 2601.05543), EchoX (arXiv 2509.09174). The tandem family offers a different answer: leave the speech model small and let a larger model or a retriever supply the knowledge while the speech model keeps talking (arXiv 2510.02327, arXiv 2604.12928).

Inference

Inference Half-cascade or Thinker-Talker with a frozen or lightly tuned LLM looks like the pragmatic route to keep text-level quality. A tandem keeps full-duplex timing and borrows knowledge from a larger model, at the price of an interface between the two and a wait for the slow side. Native interleaved is the frontier.

In the interview, say:

"I think of the LLM as swappable. The real design choice is how text and speech are coupled in time: sequentially, by a Thinker-Talker handoff, interleaved, as parallel streams, or as a small talker paired with a large thinker."
