Full-duplex and backchannelling

Full duplex means the model listens and speaks at once, so it can overlap, interrupt and backchannel without explicit turn logic. This is the target capability for the Synthesia voice role.

Schemes

Scheme Models Mechanism
Dual-stream Moshi (arXiv 2410.00037), dGSLM (arXiv 2203.16502) Own and user audio as parallel streams, no explicit speaker turns. dGSLM is textless, trained on 2000 h of two-channel Fisher audio, and generates speech, laughter and other paralinguistic signals in both channels
Time-multiplexed / flattened OmniFlatten (arXiv 2410.17799) One flattened sequence; three stages: modality alignment, half-duplex, full-duplex; backbone architecture unchanged
State head Freeze-Omni (arXiv 2411.00774), MinMo (arXiv 2501.06282) Freeze-Omni: chunk-wise state prediction on the LLM's hidden state. MinMo: a duplex predictor plus a duplex alignment stage, about 8B parameters, 1.4M h of speech
Shared timeline MiniCPM-o 4.5 (arXiv 2604.27393) 9B parameters; "Omni-Flow" aligns omni-modal inputs and outputs on a shared temporal axis; runs on edge devices with under 12 GB RAM (authors)
Token-level state BayLing-Duplex (arXiv 2606.14528) One autoregressive LLM decides when to listen, speak and stop, with no auxiliary turn-taking module. Only a few special tokens are added to the vocabulary. Started from the public GLM-4-Voice checkpoint, fine-tuned on 400K duplex samples plus a light DPO stage
Parallel output streams with tools NemotronLabs VoiceChat (arXiv 2609.21967) Streaming speech encoder and decoder-only LM with parallel output streams for agent text and structured function calls. An auxiliary RNN-T branch gives incremental user transcription, and a streaming TTS decoder speaks
Interaction layer plus thinking layer DuplexOmni (arXiv 2606.09186) An end-to-end audio and video interaction model speaks in real time; a pluggable thinking layer, running asynchronously, does reasoning and tool use
Cascade-duplex DuplexCascade (arXiv 2603.09180) VAD-free cascade that aims for duplex behaviour while keeping cascade strengths

Reported Self-reported results. The DuplexSLA authors report 98.33% backchannel accuracy on their own benchmark, against 40% or less for the closed-source baselines they tested (arXiv 2605.20755). BayLing-Duplex's authors report 92% turn-taking success and 100% interruption success on InstructS2S-Eval, and a speech-response score of 3.39 against 2.17 for Moshi (arXiv 2606.14528). NemotronLabs VoiceChat's authors report 100% takeover after user interruptions and a 4.33/5 post-interruption response-quality score on Full-Duplex-Bench 1.0, a 93% resume rate after backchannels on v1.5, and a 55.1 normalized VoiceBench average (arXiv 2609.21967). NVIDIA's PersonaPlex model card lists a smooth turn-taking rate of 0.908 and latencies of 0.170 and 0.240 on Full-Duplex-Bench, without units on the card (model card; paper arXiv 2602.06053).

Tool calls from a speech model

A voice agent that books, searches or looks up needs a tool call inside the conversation. Four ideas appear in the sources.

  • A dedicated output stream for function calls, parallel to the agent text, so the model can call a tool while it keeps talking (arXiv 2609.21967). On Full-Duplex-Bench 3.0 the authors report 82.5% tool-selection F1 and say argument accuracy and end-to-end tool execution remain areas for improvement.
  • Speculative queries: predict the tool query while the user is still speaking. Stream RAG reports QA accuracy from 11.1% to 34.2% absolute and 20% lower tool-use latency on AudioCRAG (arXiv 2510.02044).
  • Asynchronous retrieval that hides inside the gap before the answer's core content (arXiv 2604.12928).
  • A separate thinking layer that owns reasoning and tools while the interaction layer keeps the floor (arXiv 2606.09186).

First-party model pages show the same pattern in products. The Gemini 3.8 Live page lists asynchronous NON_BLOCKING function calling as the default, with SILENT, WHEN_IDLE and INTERRUPTED scheduling (model page). The gpt-realtime-2 page lists function calling and configurable reasoning effort, and warns that higher effort can increase latency (model page).

Benchmarks

  • Full-Duplex-Bench v1 (arXiv 2503.04721): pause handling, backchanneling, turn-taking, interruption management; automatic metrics.
  • v1.5 (arXiv 2507.23159): overlap scenarios (user interruption, user backchannel, talking to others, background speech), stop and response latency. It finds two strategies: responsive and floor-holding.
  • Full-Duplex-Bench-v3 (arXiv 2604.04847): real human audio with five disfluency categories and chained API calls across four task domains, run on six configurations (GPT-Realtime, Gemini Live 2.5, Gemini Live 3.1, Grok, Ultravox v0.7 and a Whisper, GPT-4o, TTS cascade). GPT-Realtime leads Pass@1 at 0.600; Gemini Live 3.1 is fastest at 4.25 s but has the lowest turn-take rate at 78.0%; the cascade has a perfect turn-take rate and the highest latency at 10.12 s. Self-correction handling and multi-step reasoning under hard scenarios are the most consistent failures.
  • CoDeTT (arXiv 2603.25434): a context-aware benchmark that frames turn-taking as a structured decision problem with fine-grained decision categories; the authors observe substantial performance disparities across decision types and scenarios.
  • ECHO (arXiv 2609.17360): paired Chinese yield-or-keep turn-taking benchmark; the authors find three of four systems over-yield on backchannels.
  • VoiceBench (arXiv 2410.17196): multi-faceted evaluation of LLM-based voice assistants.
  • URO-Bench (arXiv 2502.17810): open-source spoken dialogue models lag behind their backbone LLMs on instruction following.

Evaluation is maturing but not comparable across papers.

Data is the bottleneck

Source Detail
Fisher About 2,000 h of two-channel telephone conversation, 8 kHz; Moshi upsamples it to 24 kHz (arXiv 2410.00037)
Synthetic dialogue via TTS LLaMA-Omni 2 (200K dialogues, arXiv 2505.02625), Hibiki (aligned synthetic data, arXiv 2502.03382), Freeze-Omni (60,000 text Q&A, arXiv 2411.00774), SyncLLM (212k h synthetic plus 2k h real, arXiv 2409.15594)
Unlabelled audio Kimi-Audio more than 13M h (arXiv 2504.18425); MinMo 1.4M h (arXiv 2501.06282); Moshi 7M h (arXiv 2410.00037)

A 2026 survey (arXiv 2606.19453) proposes an L0-L3 hierarchy and an IDLE/LISTEN/SPEAK/WAIT/DUAL state machine. It finds that observed behaviour is constrained by the interaction patterns in training and evaluation, and that public training-data coverage is limited. A 2025 survey (arXiv 2509.14515) names synchronous data scarcity, architectural divergence and evaluation gaps as the main challenges.

In the interview, say:

"Architecture is mostly solved: dual-stream, state-head or plain special tokens. The bottleneck is dual-channel conversational data with real overlap and backchannels, and a benchmark everyone shares."
