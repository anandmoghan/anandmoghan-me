Diffusion and flow matching for speech

Flow matching is the objective behind many recent TTS systems. It plays two roles: fully NAR TTS, and the acoustic decoder after an AR LM. It is not the only recent route: diffusion language models denoise blocks of discrete codec tokens (see Diffusion language models and block diffusion).

In speech, ZipVoice targets the CFG cost (arXiv 2506.13053). Lipman et al. show OT paths train and sample faster than diffusion paths (arXiv 2210.02747).

Inference, why flow matching won:

A plain regression target with no noise schedule or SNR weighting. Straighter paths mean fewer steps and better RTF. Infilling is natural (Voicebox, E2).

Where it sits

Role Models
Fully NAR TTS Voicebox (arXiv 2306.15687), E2 TTS (arXiv 2406.18009), F5-TTS (arXiv 2410.06885; the authors report RTF 0.15, trained on 100K h), ZipVoice (arXiv 2506.13053; the authors report 3x smaller and up to 30x faster than a DiT-based flow-matching baseline)
Diffusion, not flow DiTTo-TTS (latent diffusion DiT, 790M parameters, 82K h; arXiv 2406.11427); Seed-TTS DiT (fully diffusion-based NAR variant; arXiv 2406.02430)
Decoder after AR LM CosyVoice 2 (arXiv 2412.10117), GLM-4-Voice (conditional flow matching plus HiFi-GAN; arXiv 2412.02612), Kimi-Audio (chunk-wise flow matching from 12.5 Hz tokens to 50 Hz mel, then a BigVGAN vocoder; arXiv 2504.18425)

Counterexamples and traps

Do not mislabel these as flow matching:
  • Qwen3-Omni: the Talker emits AR codec frames with an MTP module; a lightweight causal ConvNet (Code2Wav) makes the waveform. Not flow. Qwen2.5-Omni used a sliding-window DiT (arXiv 2503.20215).
  • MiniMax-Speech has a "Flow-VAE" (arXiv 2505.07916). Do not call it flow matching.
  • MaskGCT: mask-and-predict in both stages (arXiv 2409.00750).

ASR has little flow work. Drax is the clearest, using discrete flow matching for NAR ASR (arXiv 2510.04162).

Say this in the interview:

"Flow matching is regression onto a velocity field, so training is simple and few-step sampling is natural. In S2S it is mostly the acoustic renderer behind an AR content model."
