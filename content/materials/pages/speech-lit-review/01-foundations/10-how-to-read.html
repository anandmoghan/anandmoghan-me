How to read this page

Scope: how to model speech-to-speech (S2S) systems, treating the reasoning LLM as a downstream component. It covers newer-generation models and strategies in ASR and speech generation, plus a system design for Synthesia.

Each section is one topic, at most two printed pages, and ends with a "Say this in the interview" line. The cheat sheet in the last chapter is the one-page recall aid.

Grounding legend

Every model detail and number on this page is traced to a research paper (cited inline as arXiv IDs) or to the vendor's own page (linked inline). Numbers that the authors or vendor measured on their own systems are phrased as "the authors report".

  • Reported A number from the authors' or vendor's own first-party source.
  • Inference Design opinion, not fact.

Three traps

Three traps to avoid out loud
  • MiniMax-Speech introduces a "Flow-VAE" (arXiv 2505.07916). Do not read the name as flow matching.
  • Qwen3-Omni's waveform decoder is a lightweight causal ConvNet over a multi-codebook codec (arXiv 2509.17765). It is not a DiT or flow model.
  • MaskGCT is mask-and-predict in both stages (arXiv 2409.00750). Do not call it flow matching.
