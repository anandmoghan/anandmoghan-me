Multimodal LLM architecture

Open multimodal LLMs (Qwen3-VL, InternVL3.5, Llama 4) share one template: a vision encoder, a token-reducing connector, and a decoder LLM with multimodal positional encoding. The differences are in positional encoding, visual token count and MoE. Each claim below is cited to the model's technical report, its arXiv paper or Meta's own post.

Encoder, connector, LLM

  • Qwen3-VL uses DeepStack to integrate multi-level ViT features, plus an enhanced interleaved MRoPE and explicit text timestamps for video. It has dense 2B/4B/8B/32B and MoE 30B-A3B and 235B-A22B variants, with native 256K context (arXiv 2511.21631) Confirmed.
  • Its vision encoder is SigLIP-2, a two-layer MLP merger compresses visual features into tokens, and DeepStack routes visual tokens from several ViT layers into corresponding LLM layers through lightweight residual connections (arXiv 2511.21631) Confirmed.
  • InternVL3.5 compresses 1024 visual tokens per image patch to 256 with a pixel shuffle module. Its Visual Resolution Router (ViR) dynamically adjusts the resolution of visual tokens (arXiv 2508.18265) Reported.
  • Llama 4 uses a MetaCLIP-based vision encoder trained separately in conjunction with a frozen Llama, with MoE (Scout: 17B active, 16 experts, 109B total; Maverick: 17B active, 128 experts, 400B total; Meta's Llama 4 post) Reported.

Positional encoding

Video is handled as time-indexed positions. That one choice enables audio-video sync and streaming Inference.

  • Qwen3-Omni's TM-RoPE splits rotary dimensions into temporal, height and width (24/20/20 angles). Audio gets one temporal ID per 80 ms. Video frame IDs are adjusted to real timestamps (arXiv 2509.17765 section 2.3) Confirmed.

Early vs late fusion

Meta describes Llama 4 as using early fusion to integrate text and vision tokens into a unified model backbone (Meta's Llama 4 post) Reported.

Source conflict

The Qwen3-Omni paper (arXiv 2509.17765) is inconsistent on the Talker. Section 2.1 says the Talker no longer consumes the Thinker's high-level text representations; the Figure 2 caption says it receives high-level representations directly from the Thinker. Do not state either version as settled.

Omni models and time alignment

Qwen3-Omni is a Thinker-Talker MoE (arXiv 2509.17765). The AuT audio encoder runs at a 12.5 Hz token rate. The Talker generates one codec frame per step, an MTP module produces the residual codebooks, and a lightweight causal ConvNet (Code2Wav) renders the waveform Confirmed.

Training and RL

  • InternVL3.5 uses Cascade RL: offline RL for stable convergence, then online RL for refined alignment (arXiv 2508.18265) Reported.
  • A study of R1-like training for vision-language models reports that SFT before RL can undermine RL by inducing imitated "pseudo reasoning paths". Its GRPO variant with a mixed perception and cognition reward fostered more adaptive reasoning (arXiv 2504.11468) Reported.

Streaming video understanding

StreamingVLM (arXiv 2510.09608) keeps a fixed-size KV cache of attention sinks, recent vision tokens and recent text tokens. It uses contiguous RoPE. It runs at up to 8 FPS on one H100 on a Qwen2.5-VL-7B base Confirmed. Authors report a 66.18% win rate against GPT-4o mini on Inf-Streams-Eval and +4.30 on LongVideoBench Reported. Related: ReKV (arXiv 2503.00540) stores video KV caches in RAM and disk and retrieves only the query-relevant ones.

Trap

What an avatar that perceives the user needs

Inference
  • A streaming encoder with a fixed KV cache, as in StreamingVLM.
  • Audio and video timestamps on one common clock, as in absolute-time IDs.
  • An explicit latency budget; Qwen3-Omni suggests sub-second first packets are feasible, cold start only.
  • Emotion perception needs separate cited work; none was found here. Evaluating whether a model uses the video at all is now possible: VideoFDB (arXiv 2605.30256) reports that models often ignore the video stream and fall back to generic captioning.
  • A split between a fast interaction layer and a slower thinking layer: DuplexOmni (arXiv 2606.09186) handles streaming audio and video in an asynchronous interaction layer, with a pluggable thinking layer for reasoning and tool use. The abstract gives no numbers.

Qwen3-Omni's Talker is the closest published example of conditioning generation on multimodal understanding features.
