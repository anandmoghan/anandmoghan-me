How audio and ASR text meet in the LLM

  • Prefix: projected audio frames, then a task prompt, then text; text is predicted given the audio (Qwen2-Audio, arXiv 2407.10759; Voxtral, arXiv 2507.13264).
  • Chunk-interleaved: audio chunk, its text, then a $ end-of-chunk token; 1.28 s chunks, CTC forced alignment for targets (SpeechLLM-XL, arXiv 2410.03752).
  • Per-frame wait: after each 240 ms embedding the model emits zero or more words, then BLANK (Speech ReaLLM, arXiv 2406.09569).
  • Transducer: the LLM is the non-blank predictor; encoder frames enter through the joint network (Transducer-Llama, arXiv 2412.16464).
  • Time-aligned streams: per-stream embeddings summed into one vector per 80 ms step; ASR text is delayed, 2.5 s by default in DSM (Moshi, arXiv 2410.00037; DSM, arXiv 2509.08753).
  • Hybrid input: Whisper features (50 to 12.5 Hz) added to semantic-token embeddings; the Introduction says concatenated, the method sections say added (Kimi-Audio, arXiv 2504.18425).
  • Audio positions: Qwen3-Omni TM-RoPE gives audio temporal IDs of 80 ms each (arXiv 2509.17765).
In the interview, say:

"Offline models put audio frames first and decode text after; streaming models interleave chunks, emit a blank per frame, or sum time-aligned streams per 80 ms step. The trade-off is alignment: chunks and frames need CTC alignments, time-aligned streams need word timestamps. For a voice agent I want text while the user is still speaking, so I would pick chunks or time-aligned streams."
