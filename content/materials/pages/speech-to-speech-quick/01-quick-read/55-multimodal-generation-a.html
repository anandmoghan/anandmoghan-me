Multimodal generation: audio-visual diffusion and AR models

  • Inference Video backbones share one recipe: 3D causal VAE, DiT, flow matching. Examples: Wan 2.1 (arXiv 2503.20314), HunyuanVideo 1.5 (arXiv 2511.18870), Seedance 1.5 pro (arXiv 2512.13507).
  • Audio-driven avatars add audio conditioning to video backbones: OmniHuman-1.5 (arXiv 2508.19209), HunyuanVideo-Avatar (arXiv 2505.20156), MultiTalk (arXiv 2505.22647), InfiniteTalk (arXiv 2508.14033).
  • Ovi (arXiv 2510.01284) generates audio and video jointly: twin DiT towers, bidirectional cross-attention in every block, audio RoPE scaled to match 31 video latent frames against 157 audio tokens.
  • UniVerse-1 takes the other route: pretrained Wan2.1 and ACE-Step experts joined by lightweight cross-modal MLP connectors (arXiv 2509.06155).
  • Streaming distils a bidirectional teacher into a causal few-step student with a KV cache (CausVid, Self Forcing, LiveTalk); error accumulation over long rollouts is the failure mode.
  • AR image models split into discrete (VAR, arXiv 2404.02905; Emu3, arXiv 2409.18869), continuous with a diffusion head (MAR, arXiv 2406.11838; Fluid, arXiv 2410.13863), and hybrids (Transfusion, arXiv 2408.11039; Show-o2, arXiv 2506.15564; BAGEL, arXiv 2505.14683).
  • Inference MELLE, DiTAR and VibeVoice are the audio analogue of MAR and Fluid.

In the interview, say:

"Audio is moving into the backbone, and streaming is the frontier. The standard fix is distillation plus on-policy training, and multimodal conditioning is where it breaks."

Trap
