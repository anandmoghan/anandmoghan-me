System design: the unified speech LLM

  • Inference One speech model owns everything audio: streaming ASR with emotion tags, turn-taking, backchannels, barge-in and expressive TTS on one 12.5 Hz clock.
  • Inference Reasoning stays in a swappable text LLM behind a tool call: transcript, tags and turn state go in, streamed text comes back and is spoken.
  • Inference Fillers and backchannels come from the speech LLM, so the extra LLM hop is covered; the hop and two models to serve are the honest costs.
  • Inference The speech LLM, not the text LLM, yields on a real barge-in, ignores a user "mm-hm", and sends the played and heard text back.
  • Precedents Confirmed: Kyutai DSM (ASR and TTS as delays of one architecture; arXiv 2509.08753), Step-Audio 2 tool calls (arXiv 2507.16632), Qwen3-Omni Thinker-Talker (arXiv 2509.17765), Freeze-Omni (arXiv 2411.00774) and MinMo (arXiv 2501.06282) with small decision modules beside a text LLM.
  • DuplexSLA is the precedent for an action channel: 160 ms chunk clock, 98.33% backchannel accuracy, self-reported (arXiv 2605.20755) Reported.
  • Training data: unlabelled audio (Moshi 7M h, Kimi-Audio over 13M h), interleaved synthetic speech-text (spoken QA 13% to 31%; arXiv 2411.17607), and dual-channel conversation (Fisher, about 2,000 h; arXiv 2410.00037).
  • Then DPO on text tokens (arXiv 2506.21463) and timing RL (DuplexPO, arXiv 2607.07148; Kyutai, arXiv 2606.11167).
  • Inference Native full-duplex is the research track; the independent ASR, LLM and TTS cascade is the baseline and fallback.
In the interview, say:

"One speech LLM owns listening, turn-taking and speaking; a swappable text LLM behind a tool call owns content. Fillers cover the extra hop, and the cascade is the baseline and fallback, with full duplex as the next step."
