Speech understanding: input and ASR

  • Most models feed the LLM continuous embeddings from an ASR-pretrained encoder plus a small projector: Voxtral (arXiv 2507.13264), Qwen3-Omni (arXiv 2509.17765), Granite-speech (arXiv 2505.08699), Step-Audio 2 (arXiv 2507.16632), Canary-Qwen.
  • Discrete tokens: GLM-4-Voice uses a single-codebook VQ at 12.5 Hz, 175 bps (arXiv 2412.02612).
  • Codec tokens: Moshi's Mimi runs at 12.5 Hz with 8 codebooks (arXiv 2410.00037); Inference this suits duplex.
  • 12.5 Hz converges: Whisper emits 50 Hz frames, and 30 minutes at 50 Hz is a 90k-token sequence, so Voxtral adds a 4x downsampling adapter (arXiv 2507.13264).
  • Inference Downsampling to 10-12.5 Hz is the main sequence-length lever and costs fine acoustic detail, which motivates hybrids like Kimi-Audio (arXiv 2504.18425).
  • Canary-Qwen freezes the LLM and trains encoder, projection and LoRA; the card reports 5.63 mean WER and RTFx 418 on the Open ASR Leaderboard (NVIDIA model card).
Streaming ASR approach Mechanism
Chunked or causal encoder Qwen3-ASR AuT, 1-8 s dynamic window, one model for streaming and offline; authors report TTFT as low as 92 ms for 0.6B (arXiv 2601.21337)
Delayed streams Kyutai DSM: ASR is the text stream delayed relative to audio (arXiv 2509.08753)
Chunked decoder SpeechLLM-XL, 1.28 s chunks; authors report 2.7 / 6.7 WER on LibriSpeech test clean / other (arXiv 2410.03752)

Inference Chunk size and lookahead trade latency against WER.

In the interview, say:

"From my streaming ASR work: chunk size and lookahead set the latency/WER trade-off. The new twist is delayed text streams, so ASR becomes a special case of a multistream LM."
