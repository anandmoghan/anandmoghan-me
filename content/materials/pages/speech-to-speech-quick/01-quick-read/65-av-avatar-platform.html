  • Custom avatars sit behind a live consent video that must match the photo (personal avatars docs) Confirmed.
  • Inference Shared consent-gated identity and voice stores; consent is a hard pipeline stage.
  • Batch: Express-Voice, Express-Animate, Express-Eval and Express-Render, distilled to two diffusion steps (Express-2 post) Confirmed. Dubbing chain (ASR, MT, duration-controlled TTS) Inference.
  • Inference Audio-then-motion cascade in production; joint audio-video (Ovi, arXiv 2510.01284; Seedance 1.5 pro, arXiv 2512.13507) as a research track; causal few-step student for interactive.
  • Inference Interactive voice: a unified speech LLM with a text LLM behind a tool call; the ASR, LLM, TTS cascade is the fallback and baseline.
  • One clock Inference: Ovi scales audio RoPE by 31/157 to align 31 video latent frames with 157 audio tokens (arXiv 2510.01284); Qwen3-Omni assigns one temporal ID per 80 ms of audio (arXiv 2509.17765).
  • Perceiving the user: StreamingVLM keeps a fixed-size KV cache, up to 8 FPS on one H100 (arXiv 2510.09608).
  • Cross-paper Sync-C is not comparable, so build an internal benchmark first Inference.
  • GRPO on CER and NLL rewards collapsed prosody into monotone speech, so guard rewards against hacking (arXiv 2509.18531).
