Whisper in one page-half

  • Encoder-decoder Transformer, 680,000 h weakly supervised; one model does ASR, X-to-English translation and language ID via task tokens (arXiv 2212.04356).
  • Hz ladder: 16 kHz audio, 100 Hz log-mel (80 bins; 128 in large-v3), 50 Hz encoder frames (1,500 per 30 s window), 12.5 Hz after a 4x adapter.
  • Training: web transcripts filtered for machine-generated text, audio language ID checked against the transcript; large-v2 trained 2.5x more epochs with SpecAugment.
  • large-v3 used 1M h weakly labelled plus 4M h pseudo-labelled with large-v2 (OpenAI model card).
  • Long-form: sliding 30 s windows, beam 5, temperature fallback when log-probability is below -1 or compression ratio above 2.4, no-speech threshold 0.6.
  • Limits (model card): hallucination, repetitive text, not real-time out of the box; the fixed 30 s window and non-causal encoder make streaming hard.
  • large-v3-turbo cuts the decoder from 32 to 4 layers (model card).
  • Speech LLMs reuse the encoder (Qwen2-Audio, arXiv 2407.10759; Voxtral, arXiv 2507.13264; GLM-4-Voice makes it causal, arXiv 2412.02612).
  • Qwen3-Omni replaced it with AuT, trained from scratch on 20M h of supervised audio (arXiv 2509.17765).
  • Streaming the encoder: causal fine-tune (CarelessWhisper, arXiv 2508.12301; GLM-4-Voice, arXiv 2412.02612), a windowed encoder with a 1 to 8 s window (AuT, arXiv 2601.21337), or a wrapper that re-decodes a buffer (whisper_streaming, arXiv 2307.14743).
  • Look-ahead (right context) trades latency for accuracy: NVIDIA's cache-aware FastConformer reports RNNT WER 9.5% at 0 ms against 6.3% at 1,360 ms, and chunk-aware look-ahead beats per-layer look-ahead at the same latency (arXiv 2312.17279). Look-ahead section
In the interview, say:

"Whisper's recipe is right, but a fixed 30 s window and a bidirectional encoder are wrong for voice agents. I would start from a streaming encoder."
