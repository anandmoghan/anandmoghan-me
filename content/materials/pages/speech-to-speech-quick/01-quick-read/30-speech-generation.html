Speech generation: codecs, AR, flow, streaming

  • The tokenizer fixes the LM's sequence length and the quality ceiling; the trade is rate against fidelity.
  • Mimi is causal, 12.5 Hz, 1.1 kbps, first codebook distilled from WavLM (arXiv 2410.00037).
  • WavTokenizer runs at 40 or 75 tokens/s (arXiv 2408.16532); VibeVoice uses continuous 7.5 Hz latents (arXiv 2508.19205).
  • A controlled frame-rate ablation traces the reported quality cliff at 6.25 Hz to the training configuration, not a fundamental barrier (arXiv 2606.16969).
  • The dominant high-quality design is hybrid: an AR LM for content and prosody, flow matching for acoustics (CosyVoice 2, arXiv 2412.10117; Qwen3-TTS, arXiv 2601.15621; FlashTTS, arXiv 2606.09141).
  • Inference Flow matching won because it has a plain regression target, straighter paths, fewer steps and natural infilling.
  • Continuous AR (MELLE, arXiv 2407.08551; DiTAR, arXiv 2502.03930; VibeVoice) removes quantization loss but adds per-frame diffusion or flow cost, and RL is harder without discrete tokens (arXiv 2608.03215).
  • Inference Latency levers: for flow decoders, step distillation (FlashTTS 325 ms at 2 NFE; its table lists 843 ms for CosyVoice 2 at 10 NFE); for AR, lower token rate plus a causal codec.
  • Mel is one route to audio of four: codec decoder, flow plus mel plus vocoder, VAE decoder, or mel plus vocoder (tokens to waveform).
  • Flow-matching TTS is masked mel infilling given text and audio context; cloning is infilling after a reference clip; fidelity comes from sample rate, vocoder or codec, and bitrate (flow TTS and fidelity).
  • RVQ codecs are VQ-VAE-family autoencoders with a discrete bottleneck (codebook, commitment and adversarial losses, quantizer dropout), not VAEs; continuous tokenizers are the VAE counterpart with a KL term. For the LM, MusicGen's delay pattern reaches flattening-like quality at a fraction of the steps, and Moshi adds a depth transformer plus a 1 to 2 step acoustic delay (arXiv 2306.05284; arXiv 2410.00037) (codec training, RVQ token patterns).

In the interview, say:

"The LM decides what is said and how, the renderer decides how it sounds. Flow matching is regression onto a velocity field, so I can stream the LM and distil the renderer separately."
