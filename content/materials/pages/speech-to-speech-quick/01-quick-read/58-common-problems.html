Ranking, fixes and metrics are my judgement Inference.

  • LLM-based TTS can repeat or drop words; certain cross-attention heads learn the alignment and nothing forces it to stay monotonic, and CTC loss plus attention priors encourage monotonic attention (arXiv 2406.17957) Reported.
  • Sync-C and Sync-D are based on SyncNet (arXiv 2403.06421); THEval's authors report limited correlation with human evaluation and unreasonable values (arXiv 2511.04520) Reported.
  • Synthesia's documented name fix is a per-word pronunciation entry, respelling or IPA (script docs) Confirmed.
  • A 2026 survey calls a backchannel misread as a barge-in the most-cited duplex failure in user feedback (arXiv 2606.19453) Reported.
  • The Express-2 post gives no quantitative results, failure cases or latency figures (post) Confirmed.
In the interview, say:

"The Express-2 post publishes no failure metrics, so I would ask which ones you track. I would start with per-entity pronunciation, alignment-based hallucination rate, and phoneme-stratified lip-sync, because papers report that Sync-C correlates weakly with human judgement."
