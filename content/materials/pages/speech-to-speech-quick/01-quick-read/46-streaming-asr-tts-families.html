Streaming ASR and TTS: the modelling families

ASR families:

  • Chunked with an end-of-chunk token: WER 2.7 / 6.7 at 1.28 s chunks, 3.1 / 7.8 at 0.32 s (SpeechLLM-XL, arXiv 2410.03752).
  • Per-frame wait token: 240 ms frame plus 960 ms right context, WER 3.0 / 7.4 (Speech ReaLLM, arXiv 2406.09569).
  • Transducer with LLM predictor: 160 ms segments, WER 2.47 / 6.53 (Transducer-Llama, arXiv 2412.16464).
  • Delayed text stream: 2.5 s default delay, WER 1.7 / 4.3 (Kyutai DSM, arXiv 2509.08753).
  • Read/write policy on Whisper: LocalAgreement, 3.3 s long-form latency (arXiv 2307.14743).
  • One encoder, dynamic 1 to 8 s window: 2.69 offline, 3.33 streaming (Qwen3-ASR, arXiv 2601.21337).

TTS families:

  • Full text, causal decoder: 4-token packet = 320 ms, 97 ms first packet (Qwen3-TTS, arXiv 2601.15621).
  • Incremental text, interleaved: starts after 2 text tokens, 0.06 s against 0.22 s (SyncSpeech, arXiv 2502.11094).
  • AR plus chunk-wise causal flow: four masks and N = 5 : M = 15 in CosyVoice 2 (arXiv 2412.10117).
  • Multi-token prediction: audio delay 0 against 1 (Moshi) and 13 (GLM-4-Voice); first chunk 236 to 53 ms (VITA-Audio, arXiv 2505.03739).
  • Fixed dialogue ratio: LLaMA-Omni 2 first speech chunk about 583 ms (arXiv 2505.02625).
In the interview, say:

"For listening I would take a delayed stream or chunked ASR. For speaking I would take interleaved text and speech, because the text arrives token by token from the reasoning LLM. Chunk length or delay is the dial that trades WER for latency."
