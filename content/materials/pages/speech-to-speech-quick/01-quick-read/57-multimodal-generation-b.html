Multimodal generation: tokenizers and multimodal LLMs

  • Inference The tokenizer matters more than the generator: the consensus is a causal, heavily compressed latent regularised toward semantic features.
  • Inference Discrete codecs stay the default for audio LMs; continuous VAE latents stay the default for diffusion and flow.
  • Discrete examples: MAGVIT-v2 LFQ (arXiv 2310.05737), FSQ (arXiv 2309.15505), DAC (arXiv 2306.06546); continuous examples: Wan VAE (arXiv 2503.20314), Stable Audio Open AE (arXiv 2407.14358).
  • Best reconstruction is not best downstream: DASB reports discrete audio representations are less robust than continuous ones (arXiv 2406.14294).
  • AVTok is a 1D joint audio-video tokenizer (arXiv 2606.30811, Reported); VideoPoet concatenated MAGVIT-v2 and SoundStream vocabularies instead (arXiv 2312.14125).
  • Open multimodal LLMs share one template: vision encoder, token-reducing connector, decoder LLM (Qwen3-VL, arXiv 2511.21631; InternVL3.5, arXiv 2508.18265).
  • Qwen3-Omni's TM-RoPE gives audio one temporal ID per 80 ms and adjusts video frame IDs to real timestamps; Qwen2.5-Omni interleaved audio and video in fixed 2 s chunks (arXiv 2509.17765, arXiv 2503.20215).
  • StreamingVLM keeps a fixed-size KV cache and runs at up to 8 FPS on one H100 (arXiv 2510.09608).

"The tokenizer sets the ceiling more than the generator does. For an avatar that perceives the user I want time-aligned positions and a bounded streaming KV cache."

Trap

These latencies are theoretical figures, not end-to-end measurements. The Qwen3-Omni paper is inconsistent on whether the Talker consumes the Thinker's text representations, so do not state either version as settled.
