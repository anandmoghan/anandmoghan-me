Speech-to-speech systems: architectures, duplex, multilingual

  • Six coupling families, ordered by how tightly speech is fused into the LLM: cascaded, half-cascade, Thinker-Talker, interleaved, parallel streams, codec-free.
  • Speaking costs reasoning quality; causes reported are catastrophic forgetting and modality inequivalence (arXiv 2509.14930).
  • Mitigations: a frozen LLM (arXiv 2411.00774), cross-modal distillation from a text teacher (arXiv 2509.14930), layer-wise hidden-state alignment with RL (arXiv 2601.05543).
  • Inference Half-cascade or Thinker-Talker with a frozen or lightly tuned LLM is the pragmatic route; native interleaved is the frontier.
  • Duplex schemes: dual-stream (Moshi, arXiv 2410.00037; dGSLM, arXiv 2203.16502), time-multiplexed (OmniFlatten, arXiv 2410.17799), state head (Freeze-Omni; MinMo, arXiv 2501.06282), shared timeline (MiniCPM-o 4.5, arXiv 2604.27393), cascade-duplex (DuplexCascade, arXiv 2603.09180).
  • Data is the bottleneck: Fisher is about 2,000 h of two-channel telephone speech (arXiv 2410.00037); unlabelled audio scales (Kimi-Audio more than 13M h, arXiv 2504.18425).
  • Benchmarks: Full-Duplex-Bench (arXiv 2503.04721) and v1.5 (arXiv 2507.23159), VoiceBench (arXiv 2410.17196), URO-Bench (arXiv 2502.17810); evaluation is maturing but not comparable across papers.
  • Multilingual is lopsided: Qwen3-Omni has text in 119 languages, speech understanding in 19 and speech generation in 10 (arXiv 2509.17765).
  • Text streams serve as supervision, scaffold, alignment and capability transfer.

Reported The DuplexSLA authors report 98.33% backchannel accuracy on their own benchmark vs 40% or less for the closed-source baselines they tested (arXiv 2605.20755). NVIDIA's PersonaPlex model card lists a smooth turn-taking rate of 0.908 and latencies of 0.170 and 0.240 on Full-Duplex-Bench, without units (model card).

In the interview, say:

"Architecture is mostly solved: dual-stream or state-head. The bottleneck is dual-channel conversational data with real overlap and backchannels, and a benchmark everyone shares."

Trap
