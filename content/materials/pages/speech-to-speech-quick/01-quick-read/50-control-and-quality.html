Control and quality: steering, post-training, evaluation

  • Five control channels, usually combined: reference clip, natural-language instruction, inline tags, duration targets, style guidance at sampling time.
  • IndexTTS2 takes timbre from one prompt and emotional tone from a style prompt, so they can come from different speakers, and offers explicit-token-count duration control (arXiv 2506.21619).
  • The IndexTTS2 authors motivate exact durations by video dubbing; Inference this is the hook for avatar sync.
  • Inline tags: ElevenLabs bracket tags such as [whispers], less reliable for deliveries the voice was not trained on (ElevenLabs docs).
  • Post-training uses WER and speaker-similarity rewards: DiffRO scores codec tokens with no waveform in the loop (arXiv 2505.17589), F5R-TTS applies GRPO to flow outputs (arXiv 2504.02407), GROW does on-policy RL on the flow-matching objective (arXiv 2608.03215).
  • The recurring failure is reward hacking: GRPO on CER and NLL rewards collapsed prosody into monotone speech; iterative DPO with a few hundred human preference pairs per round fixed it (arXiv 2509.18531).
  • RRPO reports that DiffRO-style reward models can be exploited with acoustic artifacts (arXiv 2512.04552).
  • Inference Always pair automatic rewards with a naturalness term and listening tests.
  • Synthesia Express-2 (4 Sep 2025) pairs Express-Voice with Express-Video; the blind-test result is a company claim with no scores (Synthesia post) Reported.
  • Speech LLMs take several prompts at once: persona text and a voice clip (PersonaPlex hybrid system prompt, arXiv 2602.06053), a style instruction (gpt-4o-mini-tts instructions), inline tags, numeric parameters, and the user's audio. Prompting speech LLMs
  • Instruction following is the weak spot: the best of six full-duplex systems reaches only 64.4% adherence on turn-taking instructions (Instruct-FD, arXiv 2607.20460).

In the interview, say:

"I would use WER and SIM rewards plus an emotion or naturalness term, watch for monotone prosody as a hacking signature, and gate releases on human listening tests."
