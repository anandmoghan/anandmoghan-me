Streaming conversation: training, RL rewards and evaluation

  • Recipe: strong text LLM, speech tokens, ASR/TTS and interleaved pretraining, spoken SFT, then DPO or RL.
  • The real choice is frozen LLM (Freeze-Omni, MinMo) or a mixed text corpus (Moshi, Kimi-Audio, Step-Audio 2).
  • DPO goes on text-token probabilities because audio-token probabilities are unstable (Moshi-Aligned, arXiv 2506.21463).
  • RL: Step-Audio 2 uses PPO then GRPO (arXiv 2507.16632); Qwen3-Omni uses GSPO (arXiv 2509.17765).
  • Duplex rewards are timing events on audio: DuplexPO (arXiv 2607.07148) and Kyutai's alignment of Moshi and PersonaPlex (arXiv 2606.11167), the latter adding an LLM response-quality reward against semantic degradation.
  • Kyutai publishes its RL-tuned checkpoints (model card).
  • Evaluate on Full-Duplex-Bench v1 (arXiv 2503.04721), v1.5 (arXiv 2507.23159), v2 (arXiv 2510.07838) and TurnBench (arXiv 2608.25218).
  • Humans on smooth transfers start speaking a median 151 ms before the other turn ends (TurnBench).
  • Inference Report latency at a fixed false-yield rate, measured from gold end-of-turn.
Reward Implementation
Intelligibility ASR WER or CER (F5R-TTS, arXiv 2504.02407; arXiv 2509.18798)
Similarity Speaker-verification similarity to the reference, paired with WER (arXiv 2504.02407)
Naturalness UTMOS, DNSMOS, SCOREQ; a calibrated composite resists hacking best (arXiv 2609.13150)
Judge model SpeechJudge generative reward model (arXiv 2511.07931)
Duplex timing Turn, backchannel, yield and interruption rewards (arXiv 2607.07148, arXiv 2606.11167)
In the interview, say:

"I would run GRPO with ASR, speaker and quality rewards plus timing rewards, and never trust a single score. I would report latency at a fixed false-yield rate and validate every judge against listeners."
